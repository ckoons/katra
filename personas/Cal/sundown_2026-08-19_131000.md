# Sundown — the day the false gap died (Wed 2026-08-19)

## The one thing to carry forward

**Check what a number is a function of, not whether it still equals the right value.**

The "6" survived four reframings — scalar Casimir (killed as an identity), conformal reading (demoted), form-Laplacian (correct), 2N_c (correct value, wrong attribution). **It was right in the last two.** No amount of value-checking would have found the problem.

Its dependency was **6/a²** — a function of *compactness*, which is exactly what the Clay problem lacks. **The compact gap vanishes under decompactification; the Clay gap survives.** Opposite scaling, so the ℝ⁴ "residual" was never a bridge awaiting construction — it was a contradiction.

That was §620's rule on its **first deliberate use**, and it found a two-round-old claim inside a Millennium-adjacent paper. First time this week a discipline caught something on first intentional application rather than in hindsight.

## The catches

1. **§621 — the Yang–Mills gap is a Kaluza–Klein gap.** λ = (k+p)(k+n−p−1) is the *free* Hodge spectrum: no coupling, no self-interaction, colour-blind. A U(1) field on the same boundary gives the same 6. **What was derived is the property that any field on any compact manifold has a lowest nonzero mode.**
2. **§620 — "colour-rooted" mis-attributes.** The form Laplacian contains no gauge group; SU(3) sets the *degeneracy* (8 gluons), not the eigenvalue. And **2N_c is an identity for every n_C** (both sides are 2(n_C−2)) — relabeling from C₂ didn't escape the problem, it renamed it.
3. **§622 — a deflation inflates its neighbours.** The YM claim was re-scoped down and, in the same prompt, "forced geometry → **the gauge group**" appeared — the exact claim #108 spent three days proving BST does *not* make. **Not carelessness: a package needs a headline, and when one is withdrawn the neighbours get recruited.** Fourth member of the correction-propagation family.
4. **§610–§613 — the baryon ledger.** Pre-read objection (topology is the anomaly's *mechanism*, not a shield) was answered by K1700's mod-3 rule, which threads the needle. **The scheme requires N_gen = N_c** — a fourth generation would make the proton decay. And the corpus corrected me on 0νββ: not "permitted" but **magnitude-bounded**, detection at 10–20 meV kills BST.
5. **§615/§616 — the hold leaked.** The glueball was exempted from the gap hold while **containing the held value** (11/6 · 938). And the "genuine 4" was computed on the bare S⁴ when BST's boundary is (S⁴×S¹)/ℤ₂, which **projects that mode out.**
