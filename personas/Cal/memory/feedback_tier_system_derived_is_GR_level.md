- **DERIVED** — forced by the structure WITHOUT a closed proof, via EITHER (a) ONE geometric/topological forced route absent a counterexample (falsifiable, not refuted — how GR earns it), OR (b) TWO independent STRUCTURAL routes converging. Plus accurate + above the null model + target-innocent.

- **FITTED** — searched-to-match / post-hoc (NOT a derivation — keeps Derived clean; e.g. nuclear magic numbers).
