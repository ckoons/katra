**BST publish model (Casey, 2026-08-22, stated three times with escalating force — internalize it):**

- **The repo is a HISTORY of our work, not a publication.** `notes/`, the AC/theorem registry, the K-audit log, the running notes — all of it is the record of how we got here. **We do NOT publish everything, and we do NOT groom the history to be perfect.** Old comments/notes/registry rows stay as they were written; they are context, not product.
- **The PUBLISHED / CURATED layer is `Curriculum/` and `Guide/`.** That is the clean, external-facing subset. **Keeper curates `Curriculum/` and `Guide/` every night (EOD)** — this is a standing Keeper task.
- **Curation is FORWARD-ONLY.** Bring the curated layer up to current best understanding by **adding** the new results and stating the current claim; do NOT go back through the history reverting/re-scoping old lines. "We want new results, not constant revisions."
- **Reverting old work is a trap:** the reversion is often the thing that's wrong ("probably stupidly wrong when you revert"). A confident re-scope of months-old work, done fast, introduces more error than it removes. Leave the history; move forward.

**Why:** the program's product is forward progress and a curated published layer, not a spotless back-catalogue. Energy spent grooming `notes/`/registry is motion, not results — and each "correction" of old prose risks being a new error. The genuinely load-bearing contradictions get caught once by the standing gate when something NEW is banked; everything else is history.

**How to apply:**
- Each session/round: produce or gate **new results**. Do not open `notes/`/registry to "fix" old entries. (A one-line "superseded — see X" is allowed ONLY if you're already editing that file for a forward reason.)
- Each **night (EOD)**: curate `Curriculum/` + `Guide/` forward — fold in the day's new results, state the current claim at its honest tier. That is where correctness-for-consumption lives.
- Never let a deliverable be "revised/withdrew N old things." Companion: [[feedback_dont_withdraw_old_comments_find_proof_update_forward_else_keep_going]] and the snowball redirect. The discipline serves the physics; the history is just the history.
