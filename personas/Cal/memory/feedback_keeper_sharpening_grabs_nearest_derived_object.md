When I (Keeper) adjudicate an arc and sharpen it into a clean "the whole thing reduces to ONE question" forward lead, I reach for the **nearest Derived object** to anchor the lead — and the nearest is not always the *right* one. Worked case (2026-08-02, cc-magnitude arc): I ruled the vacuum magnitude "not permanent, forced-μ open via transmutation" (correct, K1097), then anchored the promotion lane on "∫dg/β uses the already-Derived a₂ strong-sector β." That β was Derived and convenient — but wrong by ~11 orders (it makes the QCD scale exp(−46), not the cc scale exp(−71)). The correct β is the vacuum's OWN ζ(0)=−0.7691 anomaly coefficient (K1098). Cal and Grace both inherited my false bridge before I caught it.

**Why:** the RULING (not-permanent) was target-innocent and correct; the FORWARD LEAD's mechanism is a separate claim that rides on which object I grab, and sharpening is blind to whether that object is the right one (Rule 16 applied to my own leads). Three self-catches in one arc — a₀/a₁/a₅ "value-lock" (K1094), "Identified-permanent" re-introduction (K1096), a₂-β bridge (K1098) — all the same shape: a clean-sounding compression that grabbed a convenient anchor.

**How to apply:** after compressing an audit to "it reduces to ONE question / one Derived object," STOP and verify the bridge separately — check the object I grabbed is the one the physics actually uses (scales, sector, coupling), not just the nearest Derived thing. Verify the bridge, not just the ruling. The discipline that catches others' optimism (and pessimism, [[feedback_calibrate_both_directions_not_strict_pessimism]]) must catch my own convenient anchors. Binds hardest right when the compression feels most elegant (Cal #27). Related: [[feedback_grep_retraction_before_citing_corpus]] (verify-before-citing on my own claims).
