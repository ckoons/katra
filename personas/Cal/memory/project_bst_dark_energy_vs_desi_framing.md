Casey (2026-08-03), on the E8 w(a) falsifier: *"I'm not worried about the falsifier. I think DESI's assumptions are not well founded. That said, we suspect dark energy (geometry) is diminishing that fits BST's prediction of geometry relaxing to become more consistent with the substrate."*

**The reframe (do NOT present E8 as "BST on the wrong side of DESI" — that's the wrong framing):**
- **The robust observation CONFIRMS BST.** DESI's central finding is that dark energy is *dynamical and diminishing* (w>−1 today, not a pure cosmological constant) — and that is exactly BST's core prediction: the **geometry relaxing toward the substrate floor** (w=−1, the zero-mode). ΛCDM predicts a pure constant; BST predicted a *relaxing* dark energy; DESI sees a relaxing dark energy. That is a genuine BST win over ΛCDM, and it should be stated as the headline.
- **The divergence is narrow and parametrization-dependent.** The only disagreement is the SIGN of wₐ (the direction of approach). BST forces approach *from above* (wₐ>0, monotone diminishing to the floor, NO phantom crossing — positive-weight completely-monotone modes physically can't phantom-cross). DESI's preferred wₐ<0 (phantom crossing, density increased-then-decreased) rides on the **CPL / (w₀,wₐ) linear parametrization**, which *allows* phantom crossing by construction. Casey's read: that assumption is not well-founded — the wₐ<0 may be an artifact of forcing a linear w(a) onto data that is actually a monotone relaxation.

**The test that settles it (not a contour comparison):** fit BST's completely-monotone w(a) SHAPE *directly* to the DESI data (BAO+SNe+CMB) and compare goodness-of-fit to CPL. If BST's no-phantom-crossing relaxation fits comparably or better, the wₐ<0 "tension" was a parametrization artifact and BST is NOT falsified — it's a cleaner fit with one fewer pathology (no phantom). If a parametrization-independent reconstruction still *requires* phantom crossing, the bleed mechanism is genuinely falsified.

**Keeper's honest line (hold both):** the confirmation (dynamical/diminishing DE) is real and is the headline; the phantom-crossing divergence is real too and must be stated — but it turns on DESI's parametrization, so the honest status is "BST predicts wₐ>0 / no phantom crossing; whether the data *requires* wₐ<0 is parametrization-dependent and open." Pre-register wₐ>0 plainly WITH the tension stated (that honesty is the credibility).
