Before a theorem proved in one dimension or parameter range is cited at BST's point (boundary d = 5; Hardy weight λ = 5/2, below the holomorphic discrete series λ > 4), pin its hypotheses verbatim from the numbered statement.

**Why:** on 2026-09-26 four citations failed this way in one day:
- Maldacena–Zhiboedov is d = 3 by hypothesis (d);
- Alba–Diab assume the higher-spin tower at d = 5;
- HPPS proved the counting in d = 2 and 4 only, and count by spin, not derivatives;
- Repka and Kobayashi Thm 8.4 need λ > 4.

Only Ørsted–Zhang Thm 5.1 (ν > (p−1)/2) and Kobayashi Thm 8.10 actually reach 5/2. See [[a-number-from-a-search-summary-is-not-a-pin]] and [[pin-from-the-equation-not-the-abstract]].

**How to apply:** when a pinning agent returns a theorem, first ask "does its d / λ range contain ours?". If it doesn't, write "conditional" or "a reading", not "restates" or "covers".
