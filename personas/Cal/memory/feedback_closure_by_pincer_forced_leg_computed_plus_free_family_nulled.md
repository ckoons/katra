α's landing C (2026-08-24) refuted the forced vertex by computation: 8π³/3, not 137. My first framing — "this closes the volume-reading class" — invited one escape: *"you computed one norm, not my ratio."*

**The permanent form is a pair, both legs citable together** (Keeper's refinement): **leg 1** — the FORCED reading (the unique no-choices normalization) is refuted by computation; **leg 2** — every UNFORCED member was already non-evidential by a null. Escaping leg 1 requires unforcing, which walks straight into leg 2. Neither leg alone suffices.

**Why:** a single computed negative kills one claim; a null alone kills only evidential weight. The pincer kills the *class*, and converts a future proponent's task from "fill a blank" into "explain away a banked number."

**How to apply:** whenever a negative is worth generalizing, ask what the free family is and whether it's already nulled. If both legs exist, say so together — the closure is permanent rather than rhetorical.
