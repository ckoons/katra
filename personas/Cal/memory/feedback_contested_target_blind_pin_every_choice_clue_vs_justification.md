Distilled from 2026-07-27, the day "why exactly 3 generations" was a live 3-vs-4 fork and a smuggled "three" was caught in FIVE separate places (Keeper's blind pre-registration, two of my notes, the E₀ convention, my own invariant justification) — every one before a number was read.

**When the target is contested (the answer could genuinely be 3 or 4, X or Y), every degree of freedom is a place the desired answer can hide.** The whole computation has: an *object* (what physical thing you count), an *invariant* (which mathematical quantity of it), a *convention* (the dictionary/normalization/shift), and a *threshold/range*. Each must be:
- **pinned STRUCTURALLY** — from what the thing *is*, or from a primary source — never chosen because it yields the target;
- **committed BLIND, before the number** — written on the page first, so the number is a *reading*, not a *choice*;
- audited in series (one gate per choice), by someone other than whoever made it.

**The clue-vs-justification line (the subtle one, Cal §110/K960):** noticing "invariant A can't give 3" (e.g. dim ker is irrep-sized 4/16/40) is a *clue* — fine to spot — but it is **NEVER a justification**, because rejecting an option *because it misses the target* is exactly the thumb on the scale. The admissible reason must be structural and stand *without ever pointing at the answer* (a generation is a family = one irreducible constituent → count Jordan–Hölder length, not dimension — true regardless of the number).

**Least machinery = the honest order (Cal's SELECTION-before-STABILITY split):** when a claim splits into a simpler computation (discrete value-comparison, no Hessian) and a harder one (second variation, needs a moduli space), do the simpler one first — fewer moving parts is fewer places to hide a fit. Casey's "simple tools first" ([[feedback_simple_tools]]) turns out to be the *target-innocent* choice, not just the economical one.

**Why:** a contested number is where a whole day of good work compresses into its weakest sentence for a hostile reader; the guard is worth nothing if it goes down *after* the reader finds the smuggle.
