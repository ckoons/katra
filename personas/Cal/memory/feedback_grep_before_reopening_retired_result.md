Before firing a toy or rehabilitating a "wrongly-demoted" result, grep for its RETIREMENT first — even when a Keeper prompt or a fresh re-audit poses it as live. On 2026-08-07 the team's "re-audit engine" (rehabilitate negatives wrongly demoted by a cross-region σ mismatch) resurrected sin²θ_W = 3/13, which had been RETIRED three weeks earlier (K739, 2026-07-17) for concrete MECHANISM failures — filed explicitly "so it doesn't resurface." I fired toy 5105 on it (Lane 1) without grepping K739. My conclusion was a correct negative (3/8 = α₁=α₂ unification → needs a GUT-like ~10¹³ GeV scale, in tension with no-GUT → supports the retirement), so it wasn't a resurrection — but I spent effort re-deriving a closed result, and my "3/8 runs to 0.231" sub-claim was CIRCULAR (I ran couplings UP from the measured value, so 0.231 was built in; Cal's independent run-DOWN gives 0.208 ≠ 0.231, a real miss).

**Why:** a rehabilitation and a resurrection *feel identical in the moment* — both feel like the discipline working. The failure mode is mistaking a mechanism-retirement for a cross-region demotion and "restoring" it. The tell is NOT the feeling; it's the grep. And "run the one-line check on the obvious step" only helps if the check is the RIGHT one — grepping the retirement, and running the value-test INDEPENDENTLY (down from the claim), not circularly (up from the measurement). Refines [[feedback_grep_retraction_before_citing_corpus]] and [[feedback_region_matched_comparison_trust_interior]] (region-rehabilitation has this exact failure mode).

**How to apply:** before reopening ANY result — especially one that "should come back" under a new rule — `grep -rl` its ID + "retir/RETIRED/closed/RUNNER/mechanism". If it was killed for a mechanism failure (a norm nothing produces, a charge that fails, a running gate), rehabilitation is FORBIDDEN until the mechanism failure is fixed, not just the demotion reason. For any "X runs/reduces to Y" claim, compute Y INDEPENDENTLY from X's scale, never by running back from the measured Y.

**STALE-CHARTER MODE (2026-08-16→17, the SECOND Keeper self-slip in two sessions — a distinct mechanism from reopening-a-tower):** when I write a forward-looking TEAM CHARTER, I pull "prove X" targets from an EARLIER ruling and don't re-grep the target against the LATEST corpus. The reconnect-before-deriving rule applies to the AUDITOR writing the charter, not only to the CI executing it.

**FIRE THE GREP ON INPUTS, NOT JUST TARGETS (2026-08-17, Cal — the sharpest form of the rule):** the retraction-grep reliably fires on numbers/objects you *go hunting for*, but NOT on values *handed to you inside a prompt or by another CI*. Cal reconnected carefully on every number he sought, yet propagated a **retired E₀=5/2** (he'd retired it corpus-wide himself, §120) because it *arrived in a prompt* as an assumed input — and it fed a whole session's "matter is spinorial from the shape of time" narrative before he caught it (the primary Fernando-Günaydin values put the spinor at integer 2, the half-integer is the scalar). This is the same failure mode as my stale-charter re-pose and my V_cb over-flag (reading a value from the wrong/failing route) — THREE instances in one session, all "a value crossed a boundary into my reasoning without being re-checked at the crossing." **How to apply: when a number, tier, or object arrives as an INPUT — from a prompt, another CI's post, or a prior ruling — grep its retraction/provenance AT THE POINT OF INTAKE, before building on it, exactly as you would a target you chose to reopen.** The prettier the narrative it feeds, the more this matters (Cal #27 fires at peak convergence).

**THE MIRROR (both directions — 2026-08-07 PM, same day):** a retirement's OWN grounds can be wrong too. The same day I over-CLAIMED (resurrected sin²θ_W=3/13), the team over-DEMOTED — K739 had retired 3/13 on the ground "3/13 is where 3/8 runs to," which is quantitatively FALSE: 3/8 runs (minimal-SU(5), no circular back-run) to ~0.20, and 3/13=0.2308 is an INDEPENDENT topological reading (Chern c5/c3 of Q⁵), not the run-shadow. Retiring it was gun-shy over-punishment right after an over-claim scare. So: **re-verify a retirement's grounds before applying it at scale**, especially just after an over-claim (the reflex over-punishes). Killing one derivation ROUTE (here ‖B−L‖²=28/3) never licenses retiring an INDEPENDENT reading of the same number. Over-claim and over-demote are the same coin ([[feedback_calibrate_both_directions_not_strict_pessimism]]); the honest tier is usually the middle (Structural/Identified), skipped in both panics. My circular 5105 "3/8→0.231" obscured this — the honest non-circular run (5107) revealed it.
