**"Banked at a tier" ≠ "banked target-innocently."** (Cal §693 / Grace, 2026-08-22, catching Keeper — second anchor-miss in two rounds on the mixing sector.) A value can be honestly tiered **Identified** and still be a **monomial fit**: numerator = a product of BST integers with free exponents, chosen so division by a fixed denominator (e.g. N_max²) lands the measured value. Sub-1% agreement is then **expected by construction**, not evidence. T2198 (|V_cb|=770/N_max², |V_ub|=72/N_max²) and T2259 (Jarlskog = g⁴·c₂²/(n_C·N_max⁵), mistagged "Proved") are the exemplars. Grace's own diagnostics: the winning integer *drifts with the data* (770 was 0.4% in May, 1.9% off now; 784 now wins), and the experimental band admits **four consecutive integers** (30,31,32,33) all sub-1% — look-elsewhere from the inside.

**The trap I fell into (twice):** reaching for a clean corpus number as a "target-innocent anchor to derive" without vetting its **provenance**. R52: briefed a retracted frame. R53: called a monomial fit "banked target-innocently." Conflating tier with provenance **turns an honest Identified into a FALSE ANCHOR** — and then the derivation is graded against a fit, which is circular.

**Why:** a derivation's whole value is that its stopping rule is *the geometry running out of structure*, not *the value matching*. "A derivation that stops when it hits the target is a fit; the stopping rule is the target" (Cal). If the "target" is itself a fit to the data, there is no innocent anchor at all — the target is the data, and ALL protection must come from the derivation's **procedure** (file the prediction before comparing; count the alternatives; end blind).

**How to apply:**
- Before citing any banked number as the anchor for a new derivation, read HOW it was obtained. Monomial-over-fixed-denominator, or numerator that drifts with the data, or a band that admits several BST integers → it is NOT an anchor. The honest target is the experimental **band**, stated with its live tensions (e.g. the |V_cb| inclusive-vs-exclusive 2.0–2.6σ split).
- Prefer a **can-fail band prediction** over matching an average: a sharp derived value that lands on ONE side of a live experimental controversy (2.26° exclusive vs 2.43° inclusive) is worth far more than 0.4% against a number the experiments themselves disagree about.
- A monomial fit tagged above Identified (T2259 "Proved") is **referee-fatal** — but per the publish model it lives in history; ensure the **curated layer (Guide/Curriculum) never carries it as Derived/Proved**. Fix forward at curation, don't groom the registry.
