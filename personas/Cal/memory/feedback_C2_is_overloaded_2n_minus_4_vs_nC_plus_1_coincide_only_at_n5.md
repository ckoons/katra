**C₂ is overloaded in the corpus and the two forms coincide only at the physical point.** The Casimir **C₂^Cas = 2n−4** (Elie 5361) and the Hilbert-polynomial shift **C₂^shift = n_C+1** (T1923; BST_1920) are different functions of n that are BOTH 6 at n_C=5 and diverge everywhere else (2n−4 = n+1 ⟺ n=5). Adopted as a standing rule, K1760, on Elie's ask — it is the single most persistent same-number trap in the corpus, caught three times (5361 killed Condition 5, resurfaced 5393, again in the Lane-A 6/5 transition 5414).

**Why it's subtle — and why NOT to over-react:** at n_C=5 both give 6, so **every banked n_C=5 result using "C₂=6" or "C₂/n_C=6/5" is correct at the physical point** — ~120 files (nuclear magic κ_ls, f_K/f_π, the baryon cascade, α-helix 18/5, QHE ratios, materials). Do NOT declare them contaminated; that's the over-reaction ([[feedback_calibrate_both_directions_not_strict_pessimism]]). The trap bites **only** when you (a) vary n / run a family sweep, or (b) assert a general-n **functional form** — e.g. calling a transition ratio "C₂/n_C" when it is really "(n_C+1)/n_C." Dressing (n_C+1)/n_C as C₂/n_C imports the n=5 coincidence and looks right only there.

**How to apply:** C₂ = 6 is fine as a *number* at fixed n_C=5 — no action. The moment a form varies n, sweeps the family, or claims a functional identity, **subscript which C₂** (C₂^Cas=2n−4 vs C₂^shift=n_C+1) and never conflate them. Grep trigger: "C₂/n_C" or "C₂ = n_C+1" inside a vary-n / transition-ratio / family-sweep context → stop and subscript. Same pattern as [[feedback_subscript_the_overloaded_symbol_int_vs_function_of_n]] (C₂^int vs C₂(n)); relatedly, pin the ratio's actual object (weight/genus vs Casimir/dim) before naming it — [[feedback_declare_the_object]].
