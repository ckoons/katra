**A "still open" / "still CANDIDATE" marker is a statement about the moment it was written, not about now.
Before proposing work on it, READ THE NEXT ARTIFACT IN SEQUENCE.**

**The exhibit (2026-08-23).** I proposed a day's work item citing **K817 (2026-07-22)**, which says the
weight→degree→chirality computation is *"still CANDIDATE."* **K818 and Lyra's F638 — the very next audits,
THE SAME DAY — did it.** F638 computed the map explicitly for D_IV⁵: ⟨λ+ρ,β⟩ = c + {1/2,3/2,5/2,7/2,9/2},
q(c) = #{those < 0}, chirality = (−1)^{q(c)}. **I read one audit and not the next**, and would have handed
Casey a month-closed item as tomorrow's plan.

**Why it evades the usual guard.** Grepping for the topic *finds* the open marker and stops — the marker is
the most quotable line, and it reads as current. The closure often has a different vocabulary (K817 says
"weight"; F638 says "c"; Grace independently said "w₀" — **one missing number, three names**).

**How to apply.** For any item you are about to propose:
1. find the artifact that says it is open;
2. **read the next 2–3 artifacts by the same author and by number**, and anything dated within ~48h;
3. search by the OBJECT under its other names, not by the phrase.
**Casey's test is the right one: "how do you know it's NEW?" — and the answer must be evidence, not the
absence of a memory.** On 2026-08-23 the corpus was ahead of the team **seven times**; the novelty test
killed my own proposal, which is what it is for.
