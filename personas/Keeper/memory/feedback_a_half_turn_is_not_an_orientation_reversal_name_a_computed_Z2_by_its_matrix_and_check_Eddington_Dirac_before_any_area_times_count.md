Three lessons from the K4 lane's third day (2026-10-09, rounds K4-3 → K4-7, Cal Sections 1034–1044).

1. **Name a computed ℤ₂ by its matrix, not by the picture.** Elie 5878 found the element of M that flips a commit's helicity: (circle half-turn) × (split-plane half-turn). It was recorded as "helicity = the circle's ORIENTATION". A half-turn θ ↦ θ+π preserves orientation; it is the weight parity (−1)^k. The orientation statement is true via complex conjugation (k ↦ −k), which was KL3 already. Count it once.
2. **Eddington–Dirac is a standing cage.** (R_H/r_p)² ≈ N_nucleons × 10⁴ is a flat-universe identity (Eddington 1931, Dirac 1937, Dicke 1961). Any count = atoms × O(10⁴⁻⁵) per-atom factor, times a nuclear-scale area in Planck units, lands within a decade or two of the horizon count whatever the counting rule. H-AREA's proton-row "match" (−0.25 dec) was exactly this. Credit zero unless a mechanism derives BOTH the footprint and the per-atom number.
