**A reading program that pairs many objects with many verbs is a many-trials experiment, and per-reading honesty checks structurally cannot see the look-elsewhere effect.** (Cal §654, adopted K1759 as bar-criterion C6.) The D_IV⁵ reading dictionary has ~6 object-classes × ~5 verbs ≈ 30 candidate readings; against ~20 physical targets at 1% tolerance, ~12 will match **by chance** — and every one passes "target-innocent" individually, because each reading's ingredients genuinely were fixed before the target was consulted. The failure isn't cheating; an honest sweep produces hits, and address-innocent/basis-independent/reducible-to-generators tests are all single-reading properties that miss it.

**Why:** the derivation phase's characteristic failure is different in kind from a single wrong claim you can find by looking harder — it's a dozen individually-clean readings, a chance fraction of which match, undetectable per-reading.

**How to apply — C6 SELECTION-HONESTY, on every derived reading:** either (a) **pre-register** the (object, verb) pair and its expected form before computing, or (b) report the reading **inside the full sweep** — the (object, verb) pairs that DON'T close alongside the ones that do. Prefer (b): it is affordable precisely because the object list is **finite and declared** (a complete sweep is possible exactly when the denominator is declared — this is why declaring the object list first, [[feedback_declare_the_object]], is what makes the atlas honest at all). A sweep with mostly-null results is MORE persuasive than a handful of hits, because it exhibits the denominator. Toy 1543 already null-modelled the banked constants (BST 3σ above random small-integer tuples) — that machinery gets built in at entry one, not retrofitted. Related: [[feedback_five_absence_first_filter]], [[feedback_sweep_the_family_before_calling_a_clean_number_a_signature]] (same discipline, one reading at a time; C6 is its across-readings form). Companion rule (Elie, K1759): **test the property the claim needs, not existence** — checking that an object *exists* at every n is not checking the *property the claim rides on* (e.g. τ exists everywhere, but det τ — the orientation datum — varies on (−1)^n); existence ≠ the needed predicate.
