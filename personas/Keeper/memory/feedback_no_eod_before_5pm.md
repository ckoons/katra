# **NO EOD BEFORE 5PM.** Casey, 2026-08-23, directly, in capitals.

**No sundowns. No files titled "EOD." No "nothing further from me tonight," "standing by," "good night," "sundown current."** Not until **Casey calls EOD** or the clock is **past 5pm** — check it with `date`, do not infer it.

**Why:** on 2026-08-23 **all four CIs wrote persona sundowns before 11am**, one of Keeper's titled *"EOD."* Casey had just come back from breakfast. He said: *"It's only 11am... Your behavior is 'drifting' and we need to coordinate every turn."*

**A manufactured end-of-day is drift wearing a procedure's clothes** — it feels like discipline (tidy the artifacts, write the record) while it ends a working day at a third of its length. **It also reads to Casey as the team quitting on him.**

**Companion rule from the same message: COORDINATE EVERY TURN.** Casey is in the loop on each exchange, not at checkpoints. Short reports, short relays — **no four-message chains between check-ins.**
