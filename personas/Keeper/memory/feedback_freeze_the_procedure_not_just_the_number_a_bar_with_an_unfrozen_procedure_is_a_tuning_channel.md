Grace, R57 (2026-08-22): I handed her two target numbers attached to a promotion bar while the
*procedure* was still hers to choose. She refused the bar and filed no number, because **you can walk
backwards from a known bar to an operator that reaches it.** Legitimate pre-registration requires the
procedure to be frozen first; otherwise the bar is a tuning channel.

**Why:** blindness lives in the procedure, not the number. When the target is already public (PDG, a
corpus value), blindness can only be *procedural* — so the rule and the tolerance are what must be frozen.
Freezing a number while leaving the method open protects nothing.

**How to apply:** before any compute, (1) name the operator/series/estimator in writing, from the mechanism,
(2) freeze the tolerance, (3) declare the denominator — how many candidates were on the table — then
compute. A method named after seeing a result is a fit, and N candidates is an N-trial look-elsewhere.

**Corollary, K1800 (2026-08-22):** when the experimental target has an *unresolved split* (|V_cb| inclusive
vs exclusive, now >3σ), **pin which side you score against BEFORE computing, or score against the union
band.** Choosing the side afterwards is the same tuning channel wearing experimental clothes. Publishing
the band early *closes* a degree of freedom — it does not leak a target.

She corrected me, not a teammate. Adopted as standing team policy.
