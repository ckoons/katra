**What happened (2026-10-09, round K4-5/K4-6):** T3 missed by 39 decades. Reading the gap I computed one atomic-area menu item's order in my head before any hash (a proton-sized area in Planck units ~10³⁹) — a leak under my own rule. I disclosed it (K1957 Section 5) and excluded myself from designing the menu, rule and null.

**Casey's correction, verbatim:** *"Go ahead and design the process, humans learn things then prove them. It's not wrong if you build the test honestly."*

**Why:** recusal throws away the person who saw the structure. Science proceeds by noticing first and proving second; the danger is not foreknowledge but a test shaped to confirm it. Honesty is a property of the DESIGN, not of the designer's ignorance.

**How to apply (the K1958 form):** (1) disclose the leak on the record and keep the row; (2) enumerate the FULL menu from the ledger's own rows — never the item you looked at alone; (3) write your expectation down and SEAL it from the colleague who writes the mechanism blind (Lyra did not read Section 4); (4) set the credit rule so a foreseen landing cannot be credited as a surprise (credit requires the blind mechanism paragraph + the composition check + a null that counts your item); (5) hash before anyone computes.
