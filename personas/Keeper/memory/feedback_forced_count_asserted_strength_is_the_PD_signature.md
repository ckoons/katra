**The relationship (a reusable derived-theory diagnostic, Casey asked for exactly this 2026-08-03):** many BST quantities decompose as **(a forced mode-count) × (a per-mode strength)**. When the COUNT is forced by the geometry but the per-mode STRENGTH is set by an *equipartition assertion* (unit total spread equally over N modes → 1/N each), the quantity is **Partially Derived — by construction.** The forced count is real and creditable; it does NOT promote the value, because the magnitude lives in the strength, and the strength is asserted, not derived.

**Why this is the signature, not a one-off:** it has now appeared in ≥2 independent places —
- **n_s (spectral index):** mode-count forced, per-mode magnitude asserted → PD.
- **Nuclear spin-orbit κ (K1133):** mode-count 12 = 6 tetrahedral edges × 2 components plausibly forced, but κ = 1/(mode-count) = 1/12 is an equipartition assertion (why unit total twist, why equal spread — unshown) → PD. The value 1/12 ≈ 0.08 is the *measured* Mayer-Jensen strength; opening the magic-number locks is how that strength was found in 1949, not a test of forcing (Cal §244).

**How to apply — the diagnostic:** when a candidate lands, split it into count × strength.
1. Is the **count** forced by the geometry, target-innocent? (Often yes — credit it, it's a real partial win.)
2. Is the **per-mode strength** derived, or is it equipartition/normalization *asserted*? If asserted → **the value is PD, full stop**, no matter how clean the count or how well it hits the target. The forced count + in-band value is NOT certification ([[feedback_no_wave_through_on_a_perfect_number]], [[feedback_universal_form_is_wood_coefficient_is_marble]]).
3. **The sole promotion path is always the same:** derive the per-mode STRENGTH (the equipartition/normalization) from first principles — and, once the team has seen the target, by someone uncontaminated, or via a forcing-chain audit of every step (Cal §245). Recording it PD in **explicit-split form** ([[reference_tier_system_PD]], [[feedback_derived_with_caveat_over_demotion]]) is honest and is usually a real state-upgrade over the prior Structural floor. This split IS the thing to name when Casey asks what "Derived" means: the count-half can be Derived while the strength-half is asserted; the value inherits the weaker half. **Refinement 2 — an INDEPENDENT LOCK substitutes for personal blindness when the whole team is contaminated (K1137):** the recurring problem is that once a target value is computed and discussed, *everyone* has seen it, so no one can be the "blind deriver" (κ was parked for exactly this). The resolution: if the forward derivation must ALSO hit a **target-innocent datum that was not used in the fit**, that independent lock certifies it just as blindness would. κ had no such lock → parked. The alpha-ladder per-bond energy ε DOES — it must reproduce the sharp ⁸Be = +92 keV above 2α, a number the 4-point ladder never used — so ε can be derived forward *by a contaminated hand* and certified by the ⁸Be number, no clean hand required. **Protocol: build forward from first principles (not factoring the known value) + certify against an independent target-innocent datum.** This is the general escape from the uncontaminated-deriver bottleneck.

**Refinement — over-determination lifts the count-half's evidential weight (Grace, 2026-08-03, K1135):** the strength of "forced count + asserted strength" as *evidence* scales with how over-determined the fit is. κ was **one number** with one free strength → the forced count carries little evidential weight (a fit opens the same locks). The alpha-conjugate binding ladder is **6 data points, 2 parameters** (bond-count law + one per-bond energy) → even if the per-bond strength ε ends up fitted, the *linear-in-bond-count structure holding across all six nuclei* is real evidence the geometry is right, not a coincidence. So an over-determined forced-count + fitted-strength lands at **Structure-Derived** (the shape is genuine evidence), not merely PD-weak; a single-number one lands at PD-weak. Same promotion path either way (derive the strength blind), but the honest floor is higher when the structure is over-determined. This is why the alpha-ladder frontier *beats* the κ frontier even though they share the exact same count-vs-strength shape.
