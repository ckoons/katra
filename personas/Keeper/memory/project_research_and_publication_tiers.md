Casey (2026-08-03) directed this as a **standing BST guideline** (`notes/BST_GUIDELINE_Research_Tiers_and_Publication_Tiers...`): keep TWO tier ladders.

- **Research Tiers** (internal, unchanged): Proved / Derived / Partially-Derived / Identified / Conditional / Structural + Proxy-Register status. Maximally honest, self-critical; here we **push for forced-by-geometry**. Keep sharp.
- **Publication Tiers** (external, GR-benchmarked, fair + accurate to a general physics audience): **Derived** / **Structure-Derived** / **Input** / **Indicative-Lead**.
- **Fixed mapping** (deterministic, no case-by-case): Proved/Derived→Derived; Partially-Derived & **Identified → Structure-Derived** (with the split-note); the named input → **Input** (stated GR-plainly); Conditional/Structural → Indicative; **LABELED-proxy/scaffold → NOT Derived** (held Indicative until recomputed — e.g. G until the a₁-on-genuine-Q⁵ closes).

**Why:** one ladder can't do two jobs. Publishing the internal audit's vocabulary misleads by **underselling** — BST is "its own worst public critic." GR is the benchmark norm: its source terms (G, Λ, w) are pure inputs with no derived structure, so BST's **Structure-Derived tier sits ABOVE where GR's celebrated source terms land**. State inputs plainly like GR states G — no apology, no "only."

**Four guardrails (what makes it honest not spin):** (1) fixed mapping, never case-by-case (no laundering); (2) research tier is the gate — publication never exceeds it; (3) full traceability (every pub claim → its research-tier record); (4) benchmark-anchored (the GR column, written down). **Interlocks with the Proxy Register** — proxy status gates the publication tier, so the system *prevents* over-claiming while fixing the underselling.

**How to apply:** Grace owns the mapping table; Cal stress-tests hostile; fold Publication tiers into the Forcing+Evidence standard + every external artifact.
