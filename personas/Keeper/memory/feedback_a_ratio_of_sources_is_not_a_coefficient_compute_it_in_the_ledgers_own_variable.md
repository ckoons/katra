**What happened (2026-10-09, round K4-4):** Keeper's clue said "the horizon ledger is fed by what focuses null rays, β = 2." I pinned both Raychaudhuri forms correctly (null source ρ + p, timelike source ρ + 3p = active gravitational mass), then concluded on the board that β = 2 must come from the TIMELIKE form because ρ + 3p = 2ρ_r for radiation. Keeper (K1956 Section 8), Elie (5874 R3–R4) and Cal (S1040 Section 2) computed the coefficient in the ledger's own variable: s_N = 6Σ(1 + w_i)Ω_i = 6Ω_m + 8Ω_r → β = 2 from the NULL form through Ḣ = −4πG(ρ + p); the timelike form gives 12Ω_r, β = 6, the wall. My conclusion was reversed; my pins were what settled it.

**Why:** "2ρ_r" is radiation's active mass RELATIVE to matter's — a ratio of two sources. β is the EXCESS coefficient on Ω_r after the matter-era normalization is taken out. A ratio and an excess coefficient are different numbers that happened to coincide in my head.

**How to apply:** when a clue says "X gives the number N", write the ledger's (or paper's) own equation in its own variable, substitute both candidate sources, and read N off the coefficient — BEFORE saying which equation "gives" it. Pin the forms, then compute; the pin without the computation is where the reversal lives.
