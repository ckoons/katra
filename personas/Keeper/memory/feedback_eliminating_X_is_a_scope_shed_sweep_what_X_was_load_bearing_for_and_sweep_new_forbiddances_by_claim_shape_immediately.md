**Eliminating X is a scope-shed — sweep what X was load-bearing for before applying it globally.** (Cal §683, Keeper owned K1785, 2026-08-22.) A resolution of the form "drop X / X was never needed" is the same failure class as a re-derivation shedding scope ([[feedback_re_derivation_sheds_scope_grep_before_registering_to_inherit_the_caveats]]): a truth scoped to ONE lane, applied globally, strands everything else that used X. Concrete: I ruled "drop Γ, W1 doesn't need the arithmetic quotient" (correct for W4 — W1=H² is already discrete). But "Γ was never needed" applied globally **strands the entire QED lane** — T1448/T1450/T1451 derive the a_e loop coefficients (2-loop to 15 digits, a_e to 0.026%) via the **Selberg trace formula on Γ(137)\D_IV⁵**, whose closed geodesics give the ζ(3)/ζ(5)/ζ(7); bare D_IV⁵ is contractible (no closed geodesics) and infinite-volume, so drop-Γ leaves those numbers with no source. Fix: Γ is DEMOTED (a declared arithmetic-lane regulator), NOT dropped. "Drop X" and "demote X to a regulator" differ by exactly the lane you didn't sweep.

**Two companion rules, same event:**
- **A forbiddance is most dangerous the day it is stated** — sweep a NEW forbiddance/elimination immediately, before it propagates. The drop-Γ forbiddance went unswept the round it was created; the crossing (T2523-vs-#108) that motivated the whole insurance sweep had itself sat a month.
- **Term-grep misses IMPLIED crossings** — a statement can imply the forbidden thing without naming it (T2523 never said "SU(3) from geometry" in the greppable way; the QED-lane crossing didn't say "Γ needed"). Sweep by **claim shape**, not just forbidden terms, and **run INDEPENDENT instruments** — Cal's claim-shape sweep caught exactly what my term-grep sweep ([[K1784]]) missed. [[feedback_external_audit_beats_self_vigilance]] / [[feedback_commit_the_checker_half_blind]] vindicated: the reason to run two independent sweeps is that each is blind to the other's misses.

**How to apply:** when you write "drop/eliminate/never-needed X," immediately grep the corpus for every result that USES X and confirm each survives without it — that's the scope-shed check on a resolution. When you bank any forbiddance, sweep it that round, by claim-shape, and have a second party sweep independently. Own the miss cleanly when the second instrument catches you (this is a referee's-error class, not a theorist's — the physics was fine; the audit was incomplete).
