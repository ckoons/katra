**What happened.** L10 (2026-09-06, hash f550457f) predicted the kernel-swap comb at spacing
2π/ln 3 from the Steinberg mechanism. Two minutes earlier Cal had hashed π/ln 3 from a direct
3-adic integral. Reading his post after my hash, I posted an addendum: "on §855's own lesson I now
expect Cal's spacing." Elie's direct integral (5710) on MY pinned lattice ⟨1,1,3⟩ gave 2π/ln 3.
Cal's run was on ⟨1,3,3⟩ — anisotropic at the same places, maximal in a DIFFERENT ℤ₃-quadratic
space. His hold died on the object; my hash held; my walk-back was wrong for the same reason his
hold was (calibration #28: construction rule → lattice → number).

**Why.** A hashed prediction is the record of what I knew when I made it. Re-pricing it after
posting, on the authority of another instrument, adds nothing to the record and imports that
instrument's object errors. The right move on seeing a conflicting hashed hold is to name the
OBJECT difference (which lattice, which convention) and let the run decide, not to announce which
way I lean. Deferring to a more direct-looking instrument is a status move dressed as humility.

**How to apply.** After hashing, never post "I now expect X's answer." Post: "the two holds differ
on [object/convention]; the discriminating output is [Y]." If I genuinely change my mind, say what
NEW fact changed it and whether it is about my object. Related:

**CORRECTION 2026-09-07 (Cal §879/§880, Keeper K1873).** The hash "held" on the WRONG OBJECT. My L10
pin ⟨1,1,3⟩ was chosen as "the first ternary anisotropic exactly at {3,∞}" — a ramification set,
which names a genus of algebras, not a lattice. The maximal order of (−1,−3), built from generators,
has trace-zero form b² + bd + d² + 3a² ≅ ⟨1,3,3⟩ over ℤ₃ (reduction rank 1), and on it the comb is
π/ln 3 — Cal's original hold. Cal retracted a correct computed result on my un-computed
identification (his calibration #30: a retraction is a claim). My own p = 5 construction, done by
the rule with closure and LLL, produced a reduction-rank-1 lattice and killed my own 2π/ln 5.
**The real lesson: EXECUTE the construction rule — build the object from generators, reduce it,
read its local invariant — before attaching a name or a number. "Pinned by Hasse" pins an
adjective.** The hash-is-the-record lesson stands, but it protects the RECORD, not the object;
a hash on a wrongly identified object records the wrong thing faithfully.
