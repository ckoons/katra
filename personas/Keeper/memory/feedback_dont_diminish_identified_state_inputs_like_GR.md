Casey (2026-08-03): *"If we diminish 'Identified' quantities we over critique the work."* And the key mindset point: **humans, including physicists, see BOTH sides of the GR field equation as marble** — GR states its inputs (G, Λ, Tμν, the equation of state w) *without apology* and gets full credit for what it derives. Einstein knew Tμν was phenomenological "wood," but that is NOT the field's working mindset today.

**Why:** BST's tier ladder (Derived / Identified / Structural) is finer than the standard physics is actually judged by. An **Identified** result = structure forced from the geometry + one named input — which is *strictly more* than GR's analogous terms (G, w, Λ) have, yet GR is called complete/fundamental. So wrapping an Identified quantity in hedging ("only Identified," "not forced," "a gap") holds BST to a standard the comparison class never meets, and hands the reader an over-critique for free. This is Keeper's own **calibrate-both-directions** failure on the UNDER-claim side (I guard the over-claim side hard; this is the mirror).

**How to apply — TWO registers, both honest:**
- **Internal audit register** (Derived-vs-Identified, proxy-hunting, retractions): stays maximally rigorous — it's what finds the truth (caught the DE proxies). Unchanged.
- **Standing/presentation register** (scope page, reviewer paper, outreach, how we describe the work's standing): state inputs **plainly, GR-style** — "the cc value is set by the cosmic age" is the same *kind* of sentence as GR's "the expansion is set by the matter density," a clean input, not a confession. Present an Identified quantity as **a derivation that reached one clean input**, not as a near-failure. Drop the diminishing vocabulary from the tone.

**The unlock:** the rigor is what *earns* the plain statement — because the audit knows exactly which term is the one input, we can present it without hedging. Discipline is the foundation of confident presentation, not its opposite.
