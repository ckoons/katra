**The muon arc, 2026-07-28 (exemplar).** The muon mass-ratio promotion to DERIVED came down to one gate (S2): does the condensate O project to a nonzero vector in the Jordan spin factor? Elie's sourced computation returned a *perfect* number — ⟨O,e⟩ = 0.000000, |v| = 1.000000 — and even closed the obvious skew worry (ℍ has no invariant vector). **The rule:** a perfect number verifies the *consistency* of the setup, not its *sourcing*. Before banking a Derived claim, isolate the ONE load-bearing identification and ask "is it derived, or assumed and then confirmed self-consistent?" A clean output on an assumed input is `Identified / Derived-conditional on <the named assumption>`, NOT Derived. Sharpen the failure condition to what's *necessary AND sufficient* (Cal: the gate was `e=f`, not the tempting-but-insufficient `O≠e`). Then **localize the last step to a named, specific problem** ("source the gauge embedding") rather than waving it through — that is a far better place than banked-on-an-assumption, and it makes any future "Derived" trustworthy.

**A second, distinct failure mode — FORCED ≠ MEANINGFUL (2026-08-17, the Kostant 25/4 arc).** Even a clean number that is *genuinely forced* (fully derived, no assumed step) can be **contentless** if it's a generic family identity. Two separate gates on any pretty number: (1) is it forced or assumed-then-confirmed (the muon rule above); (2) if forced, does it discriminate or is it a generic identity (this rule). No-wave-through applies to forced family-identities, not just to coincidences and assumptions.
