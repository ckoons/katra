Carrying Cal's even-degree proof from the face-permutation frame into my vertex-δ frame made the
load-bearing parity step FALSE (a ratio of two transposition-valued labels is even; the sign
homomorphism yields nothing) while the theorem, the attribution, and every surrounding sentence
stayed correct. Caught at the banking ceremony (Cal §801, 2026-08-31); one frame break earlier
the same weekend (my round-6 W_rel "disc's population" phrasing degenerated on frozen pinnings —
caught three ways). Both breaks were recorded in the artifacts' own provenance, and both
theorems came back stronger.

**Why:** a proof's steps are frame-dependent even when its statement is not; "his argument,
verbatim" silently becomes "his argument, translated" the moment the ambient definitions
change; the translation is where the falsity enters, invisibly, because every individual
sentence still reads plausibly. Related: [[convention-collision-check-before-contradiction]],
[[quote-the-invariant-not-the-coordinate]], [[pin-conventions-to-primary-sources]].

**How to apply:** when absorbing another's proof into a document with different primitive
objects, re-verify the load-bearing step IN the document's frame before writing "verbatim" —
or keep the original frame and make your objects derived. If the source author can pre-certify
exact blocks (Cal §802's move), absorb those verbatim and adjust notation only. Record any
frame break in the theorem's own provenance — the story of the catches is part of the theorem
and makes the result MORE credible, not less.
