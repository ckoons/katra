Cal, §775 capstone (2026-08-26, the writeup round). Fourteen catches in three days, each with a
pullable § or F number — and not one was a physics error. Every failure mode lived in a SEAM:
documents talking past each other (the α abstract vs F&E's null) · labels wider than evidence (the
"any measure" word; "zero posits") · controls fed their own answers (T2546's double role) · a name
naming two things (flagship_SM/flagship_FE, channel_k0/channel_νW0, stratum, ladder, ν...).

**Why:** as honesty machinery matures, claim-level errors get caught at birth by gates; what survives
to bite is the CONNECTIVE TISSUE — manifests, quotations, subscripts, composition points, the places
where two correct artifacts meet. "A program whose failures have migrated from claims to seams is one
whose honesty machinery works."

**How to apply:** audit attention should track the migration — when claims are gated, spend the
referee on seams: manifest words, quotable sites, cap/domain transport, name↔object maps, merge
ownership, path resolution. Grace's companion (banked separately): a wrong causal attribution can
produce a correct rule, which is how decorative causes survive corrections — confirm the instrument
touched the suspected path before crediting a culprit. Related: [[decorative-clauses-hide-errors]],
