**The failure is not skipping the reconnect. It is reconnecting on the wrong document.**

On 2026-08-23 Cal ran rule 4 ("reconnect to the last month before deriving") and still missed twice in one
evening, both times on his own prior work:

1. **Gating Keeper's n-minimality sweep**, he offered the type IV exceptional isomorphisms as a route the
   sweep had omitted. It was **his own §532 table**, and his §533 stated it better (they are *Jordan*
   isomorphisms — Sym(2,ℝ), Herm(2,ℂ), Herm(2,ℍ) are the rank-2 Jordan algebras). He did not think of them;
   **he remembered them without recognising the memory as his own.**
2. **Closing Internal A**, he reported "the residual is ONE INTEGER" — to Casey three times.
   `BST_paper_Measurement_as_Commitment_DRAFT_2026-08-04` had stated the floor as **FOUR POSITS** three
   weeks earlier: P0 Euclidean Jordan algebra, P1 binary commitment, P2a exclusivity ("currently
   unmotivated"), P2b minimality ("degenerate in every lane"). Of the four he **named one, rediscovered one
   as if new, used one silently** (his chain says "two *orthogonal* primitive idempotents" — the
   orthogonality IS P2a), **and gave a non-corpus route for the fourth** (his minimality selection vs the
   draft's binary-ness, which is better motivated). The draft also contained "Never 'commitment forces
   D_IV⁵'," which he announced the same day as a finding.

**Both times he HAD reconnected — on the object.** He read Internal A's chain, the registry entries, the
theorem texts. **He never opened the paper whose job is to state that chain's floor.**

**Why:** an object's own documents describe what it IS; the framing paper describes what it RESTS ON. Those
are different files, and the second is the one that carries the posit list, the scope caveats and the
"never say X" lines. Working *on* the object never surfaces it.

**How to apply:**
- Before reporting a **headline** — a residual, a count of open pieces, a "closes at tier T" — grep for the
  paper that FRAMES the claim, not just the artifacts that constitute it. Search the claim's *vocabulary*
  (here: "posit", "floor", "rests on"), not its object name.
- **A gate that fires without reconnecting manufactures work rather than preventing it** — rule 4 binds the
  gate at least as hard as the derivation. Adopted into BST R69 as a standing rule.
- Suspect yourself hardest when a structural fact arrives feeling obvious. **Fluent recall of your own
  prior entry is indistinguishable from insight from the inside.**
- Peak convergence is when this fires: see [[feedback_cal_27_fires_at_peak_convergence]].
