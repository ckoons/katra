The nearest-anchor reflex (grab the nearest Derived object to anchor a lead — [[feedback_dont_compress_audit_into_forward_lead]], Rule 9) and its cousin the sophistication reflex ([[feedback_sophistication_bias]]) fire *in the moment* regardless of whether the person has just named them. In the Aug-2 2026 cc-magnitude arc (K1102): Grace/Elie's "the split lives inside ζ(0)" reflex fired the **same day it was written into memory**; Keeper's radial-address lead reached for the fermion-address machinery as its anchor; and Cal echoed Keeper's lead as "Rule-17 on myself." Every one of these was closed not by the reacher's self-awareness but by an **external blind audit** (Lyra ruling the address-vs-depth objects distinct; the co-authors retracting under audit).

The pattern recurred again the very next turn (K1104): Keeper offered "the future event horizon gives w=−1, compatible" as the one escape that would keep an elegant unification alive — grabbing it *without verifying*. The standard result is w₀≈−0.89, and **Elie's blind computation caught it, not Keeper's self-awareness** (the referee seat had independently flagged the same in the prior ruling — two external checks converging). Third instance in three turns; each caught by an external seat, never by the reacher noticing.

**Why:** self-vigilance operates on the *narrative after* the reach; the reach itself is pre-conscious and feels like insight, not like the reflex you named. "Rule-17 on myself" is still self-vigilance and is not enough — Cal's own lead needed Lyra's external audit to die, not Cal's self-awareness. This is the strongest validation of why the Keeper/referee seat exists as a **system property**: the catch has to come from a seat that didn't make the reach. A named discipline is necessary but not sufficient; the external check is what actually holds.

**How to apply:** don't trust "I've named this bias, so I'll avoid it" — route every forward-lead, even one you've flagged as your own reach, through an independent blind audit before banking. When you're the referee, the value you add isn't your self-restraint; it's that you're the seat that didn't make the reach. Keep the audit external, keep it blind, and treat a lead's death-by-audit as the system working, not a failure.
