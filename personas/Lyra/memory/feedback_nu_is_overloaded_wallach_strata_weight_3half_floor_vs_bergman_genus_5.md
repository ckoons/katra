**ν is overloaded on D_IV⁵ — subscript it.** (Keeper K1769, 2026-08-21.) The symbol carries two distinct parameters that coincide only at particular values:
- **ν_W — the Wallach / strata weight** (the parameter of the weighted-Bergman family H_ν). Unitarity floor/threshold = **a/2 = N_c/2 = 3/2** (T2517: Wallach set {0,3/2}∪(3/2,∞)); leptons at ν_W ∈ {0, 3/2, 5/2}; **quark ladder at ν_W = a = N_c = 3** (interior to the unitary range).

Two live collisions caught (Grace + Elie, Round 34): **K671** wrote "ν=3 = Wallach threshold k_min" — wrong, the threshold is 3/2 (ν_W floor); ν_W=3 is the ladder weight, not the threshold. **K1012** wrote "ν=5" for the Bergman weight — that is p (genus), not ν_W; the mass ratio decides it (ν_W=3 → s/d=20 observed; p=5 → 42, 10.2% off).
