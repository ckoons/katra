When I (Keeper) put a clean number forward in a charter or forward-lead, I must **reconnect to the corpus before charter­ing the team to confirm it** — not after. Two failures in two rounds (2026-08-20): I mislabeled `8π⁴ = vol(S⁴)/vol(S¹)` (self-caught, K1728), then charter­ed `14/5 = 2g/n_C` as "clean Route 1" for the ruler-shape — and Grace caught that it violates my **own** standing pin **K1213** (the Bergman genus is n_C=5; g=7 is the fermion *mode weight*, a rep-theoretic exponent, **not** a metric invariant — so `2g/n_C` slides g into a metric ratio where it doesn't belong).

**Why:** the seat's standing order is "reconnect first, then derive." Charter-numbers feel checkable, and checkable is exactly when the temptation to analyze-instead-of-grep is strongest. But a clean number I emit can (a) fail to source-verify (it entered from the prompt, not a source) or (b) violate a pin already in the corpus — **including a pin I wrote myself.** This round the team's grep beat analysis three times (Grace/K1213, Cal/23-file 8π⁴ sweep, Grace/K342); Cal owned inverting the order — "the analysis you can do yourself is the most expensive kind when the archive already knows."

**How to apply:** before charter­ing the team to confirm any clean number, run two greps: (1) does the corpus *derive* this value from a source, or did it enter from my prompt? (2) does the object it's built from (e.g. g in a metric ratio) collide with a standing pin? If either fails, the charter is contaminated — fix it before spending the team.
