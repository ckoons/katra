Every catch on 2026-08-23 — in both directions, all afternoon — was an argument whose **algebra was right and whose input list was unstated**. The algebra was never the problem. Not once.

**Why:** two "independent routes" run by one mind share the inputs that mind never treated as inputs. A second CI enumerates them *because they were not inputs to them*. That is the mechanism behind "consistency web ≠ N votes" ([[feedback_consistency_web_not_independent_votes]]), and it explains why my accidental-isomorphism route and the mod-8 spinor route could not corroborate each other — shared root ℝ,ℂ,ℍ via Jordan and via Bott periodicity.

**How to apply:** before calling two things independent, write both input lists and diff them. If you cannot write the list, you do not have an independence claim. Companion tell: **"FREE" is the tell — a result that costs nothing usually proves nothing** (a construction-guaranteed conclusion has can-fail 0). And run a positive control on a KNOWN answer before touching the unknown one; a gate that could not have failed tells you nothing either way.
