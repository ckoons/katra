Two instrument blind spots, both caught by the team's protocol (Keeper K1835 A2; the plateau probe), both in the false-negative direction:

1. **A no-op primitive silently widens the alphabet.** `apply_move` returned the coloring unchanged when a stage was illegal, so every "family word" count from toys 5570–5586 admitted 3-swap truncations that are not family words. Tag the legality of EVERY stage of a composite move and count fully-legal-and-X, the same witness.
2. **A sampled potential mis-values unsampled members of the target set.** d_gate computed as min Hamming to a SAMPLE read 10 on an image that was itself in the gate phase; the loop never took the finishing step. Value the potential by its DEFINITION (0 on any member of the target set, checked directly), not by the sample. Every "no-descent" ever reported (J3, the six, the 77) was this.

**Why:** both errors passed step audits because they lived in the instrument's primitives, not in any claim; both moved counts only downward, so "measured everywhere" was a floor, not the number.

**How to apply:** before reporting any k/N: (a) list each primitive that can silently do nothing and assert it did something; (b) for any potential defined via a set, evaluate membership directly on every candidate, then distance. Positive-control the count with an independent hand-check of a few hits (5591's were hand-verified in a separate code path).
