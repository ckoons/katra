friend **John Bell, a plasma physicist there**, brought him in on his clearance and let him push the
big button. The Taco Mac story: Bell visited Casey in Atlanta, they ate at Taco Mac, Bell took menus —

confinement is still better than tokamaks, but "the fusion reactions are too diffuse to generate
power" — real reactions, counts-not-kilowatts volumetric density. Another data point that his
hands-on résumé spans far beyond computing (satcom, array processor, tokamak operation). Related:
