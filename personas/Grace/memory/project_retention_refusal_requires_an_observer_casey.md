**Casey, 2026-09-05:** "if by 'selection' you mean Natural Selection, you need an observer."

In the retention program the three operations are NOT symmetric. Growth and coupling are
observer-free (bonds form, vertices attach). Retention (T1) is observer-free (a property of maps).
**Refusal/selection requires something that distinguishes states — a measurement, hence an
apparatus.** Not necessarily conscious; a lion or a solvent is an apparatus. The refusal operator
is Maxwell's demon and T7 is Szilard's accounting with the demon's own cost omitted.

**Why:** every efficiency number quoted on 09-05 (the 0.50–0.65 band, later shown to be the
searcher's not the space's) left out H_obs. That was filed as contamination; it is not — the
demon's efficiency depending on the demon is the correct physics. Charging H_obs = log2(candidates)
REVERSES the policy ordering: greedy 0.517 → 0.129 net, random 0.401 → 0.401 net. Suggests natural
selection's random-variation-plus-differential-survival is an ECONOMY, not a limitation: the
cheapest possible demon. NOT SETTLED — a reversible comparison-based argmax could cut H_obs to
~1 bit per comparison, so the reversal may not survive a careful accounting.

certificates) is wrong because an experimenter outside the system can produce all of it — Lyra did
exactly that to a torus fourteen times that morning. The corrected definition: **a crystal makes no
refusals; a selected population's refusals are made by its environment; a living system makes its
own.** This makes "a record system that modifies its own move set" the central next instance, not
an optional one. Connects to [[user-casey-consciousness-theory]] and the T317–T319 observer
hierarchy in [[project-ci-persistence-track]].
