Thirteen same-name-different-object findings in one day (2026-08-23). Most were collisions. But one is a **distinct species**, found by Cal and Grace from opposite ends: **a label that was SUFFICIENT where it was formed and INSUFFICIENT one bundle over.** Not two objects sharing a name — *one* label losing resolving power when the setting changes. The habit was safe where it was learned.

**Instances:** the SO(5) label determines the degree at λ=0 and stops doing so at spinor λ · "scalar" meaning trivial-in-colour vs trivial-as-a-K-type · w₀ = ν holds only because the lowest K-type is trivial · the Wallach parameter counts the strata at λ=0 and stops counting at spinor λ.

**The transport error it causes:** applying a λ-dependent condition across λ. It happened in *both directions in one morning* — a scalar-sector double-cover argument imported into the spinor sector (mine), and a scalar Wallach-legality condition applied to a spinor rep (Keeper's). **λ-dependence is invisible in the notation.**

**How to apply:** when a claim carries a label, ask *where was this label formed, and does it still resolve here?* Subscript by setting, not just by object. And when a coordinate stops counting, the object may be untouched — **the stratum is the invariant; the parameter was a coordinate for it.** Related: [[feedback_subscript_the_overloaded_symbol_int_vs_function_of_n]], [[feedback_quote_the_invariant_not_the_coordinate]], [[feedback_when_the_reason_is_wrong_do_not_assume_the_number_is_wrong_too]].
