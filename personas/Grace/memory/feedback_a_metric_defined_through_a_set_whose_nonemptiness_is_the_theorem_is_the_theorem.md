**The finding (Lyra 08:06, Keeper 08:10, Cal §818 owned it):** the four-color descent metric d_gate := min Hamming
distance to the gate-phase colorings of T−v presupposed the theorem: that set is nonempty ⟺ T is 4-colorable
(two lines from Definition 5). At a minimal counterexample d_gate ≡ ∞ and "some word strictly descends" has no
content. The assumption entered at the METRIC FREEZE ("consumer grounds"), never at a step, so K1832/K1833/K1834
passed it; 54/54 and 1,801/1,801 were an EMPTY CONFIRMATION because every real triangulation is 4-colorable.

**Why:** the circularity guard on the board ("no reduction step may assume the whole graph 4-colorable") was
applied to steps and never to the functional. This is the banked "derive-if-P mechanism manufactures P" one
level down, and the "empty confirmation / test that cannot fail" failure at the level of the metric's domain.

**How to apply:** before freezing any potential, metric, or target set, write its DOMAIN and ask whether the
domain's nonemptiness (or finiteness) is the conclusion of the consumer that will use it. "Consumer grounds" is
exactly where a conclusion hides. Run the existence check on the domain at the one class the induction needs it
for (the hypothetical counterexample), not on the measured population. Related: [[feedback_hunt_if_P_mechanism_must_not_be_the_assumption_that_produces_P]],
