A number taken from a web-search result summary is not a pin, even when the summary names a paper. Open the primary source, or write the value as "to be pinned" and hand it to Grace.

**Why:** On 2026-09-25, K1923 cited the neutron master-formula constant as "K = 4905.7(1.7) s, arXiv:2501.17916" plus "4908(4) s, CMS 2018", both taken from search summaries. Grace found that 2501.17916 never prints 4905.7 (its equations give ~4902.3 s), and that 4908(4) appears nowhere as cited. The headline σ turned out to depend on K (0.3σ–1.8σ across six pinned values). The K1923 amendment owns it.

**How to apply:** Any number headed for a K-note, the board, or a front page either comes from a source I opened, or is labelled pin-owed. This extends [[a-number-without-a-retained-instrument-is-a-memory-not-a-measurement]] and [[pin-checksums-from-the-api-json-not-a-rendered-page-and-a-reproduction-is-numeric-to-tolerance-not-byte]].
