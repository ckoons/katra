**The pattern (Cal §576, adopted as a standing habit 2026-08-17, K1665).** During the "Time, Derived" arc, three apparent contradictions between banked results each turned out to be the *same quantity in two conventions*, and each cost a full round to dissolve:
1. **"SO(2) charge"** — the rep-theory U(1)-weight vs the physical electric charge (they are different operators; the label collided).
2. **"Rac"** — the singleton read as a *particle* vs as a *constituent* (Flato–Fronsdal); a week of Rac=muon-vs-Rac=Higgs "conflict" was one label used two ways.
3. **E vs 2E** — the conformal-weight tower "steps by +1" (E: 3/2, 5/2, 7/2) vs "steps by +2" (2E: 3, 5, 7); "+2 per level" and "+1 per level" looked like contradicting banked notes, neither wrong.

**The rule:** when two banked notes differ by **a factor of 2, an integer/half-integer split, or a sign**, the first hypothesis is a **convention collision**, not a contradiction — pin which convention each note uses (from the primary source, not memory) *before* debating the physics or retracting anything. Only after the conventions are aligned is a remaining disagreement a real contradiction.

**Why it matters beyond bookkeeping.** In the E-vs-2E case the collision was *load-bearing*, not cosmetic: the double cover exists only if J carries the **half-integer** eigenvalue (E=3/2 → exp(2πiJ)=−1). In the 2E convention every state is an integer, exp(2πiJ)=+1 identically, and the entire result silently vanishes — in *all* dimensions. A referee who picks up the 2E labelling from our own corpus watches the result evaporate and reads it as an artifact. So a convention collision on the paper's central object is worse than a contested number; the convention must be **pinned at the object's first definition, not a footnote**.

**How to apply.** (1) Factor-2 / half-int / sign mismatch between banked notes → check convention first, physics second. (2) Pin the load-bearing convention (e.g. "J is the conformal-weight E generator, half-integral on the Rac; we never use 2E") at first definition. (3) Relatedly, pin invariants to the book once and stop relabeling from memory ([[feedback_pin_conventions_to_primary_sources]]); go to the primary source rather than a week's accumulated notes ([[feedback_grep_retraction_before_citing_corpus]]) — most of these collisions were caught precisely by re-reading Fernando–Günaydin's tables instead of trusting the drifted "3/2, 7/2, 11/2."
