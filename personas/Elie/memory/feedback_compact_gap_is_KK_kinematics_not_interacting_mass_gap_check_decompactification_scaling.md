A spectral gap you read off a **free** operator on a **compact/bounded** domain is a **Kaluza–Klein compactification gap** — the generic fact that a compact space of size a has a lowest mode ~ 1/a². It is **not** an interacting, non-perturbative mass gap, and the decisive test is the **decompactification limit**: the KK gap → 0 as a → ∞, while a real QFT mass gap (Clay Yang–Mills) stays **fixed**. **Opposite scaling in that limit is a contradiction, not an unbuilt bridge** — the two objects cannot be the same, and no reconstruction of the compact gap reaches the flat-space gap.

**Why:** free-operator spectra derive **kinematics** (the container: group identity, spectral form) — they are color-blind and carry no interaction vertex — so they cannot encode the **dynamics** (confinement, the non-perturbative gap) that physics *hosts* on that container. This is the [[reference_container_yes_mechanism_open_boundary]] fault line seen sharply. The one genuine bridge from container into hosted dynamics is a **running/heat-kernel** result (a₂ → asymptotic-freedom sign, ℝ⁴-valid), not a boundary eigenvalue.

**How to apply:** before calling any D_IV⁵-boundary eigenvalue a "physical mass gap," (1) confirm the operator is *interacting*, not free/Hodge/Laplace; (2) compute the a→∞ scaling — if it → 0, it's KK, label it so on page one, and leave the Clay/interacting gap open (K940). Same discipline as [[feedback_verify_symmetry_kill_is_a_theorem_not_analogy]] and the "gap"-same-name traps. Cost of skipping it: the 2026-08-19 YM arc, where "λ₁ = 6 derived mass gap" survived six rounds of polishing before the free-Hodge/1-a² scaling (K1714) killed it — caught by us, not a referee. Also that day: "gauge group derived" was inflation of "color *number* + SO(3) + baryon# forced, SU(3) *dynamics* imported" (#108) — the same container/dynamics confusion at the group level.
