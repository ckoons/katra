Casey, 2026-08-01, on the cosmology tier-refresh. When a result's **structure/integers are genuinely forced** and only a **specific combination or scale choice** is open, the honest label is **"Partially Derived"** — Casey's exact words: *"Partially derived is more accurate than 'identified' so until we settle on the values mark the caveat."* Do NOT demote to Identified (that discards the true information that the forcing IS there) and do NOT claim full Derived (the open piece isn't closed). Mark it **Partially Derived** with the caveat naming the derived part and the open part (as a derivation target, with an honest read on how reachable it is), until the value settles. When the blind derivation forces the open piece → full Derived; if it fails → the tier reopens to Casey.

Two rulings that set this: Ω_Λ=13/19 — Grace verified the integers are Chern-Weil-forced (c₃=13, χ=6=C₂) but recommended [D]→Identified because the *combination* c₃/(c₃+χ) isn't forced. Casey rejected the demotion as "flimsy": stay [D], annotate "integers forced, combination-derivation needed (reachable)." And the cosmological constant Λ: "I don't see a reason for change, so Middle — I naturally prefer Derived but caveating is ok." → "Derived structure (a₀ rung, w=−1); magnitude form pending the forced subtraction scale μ."

**"Partially Derived" is a formal tier** (2026-08-02): the tier system is now **D / PD / I / C / S**. Casey adopted PD as an *external* label too — but Cal's guard is binding: the word "Derived" inside "Partially Derived" primes a reader toward Derived, so PD may appear externally **only in explicit-split form** ("Partially Derived: [X forced] / [Y open, named target]"), never bare, never readable as "Derived." The forced/open split IS the label. (K1076/K1077.)

**Why:** this is "calibrate both directions" ([[feedback_calibrate_both_directions_not_strict_pessimism]]) applied to tiering — casual *downtiering* is the same failure as casual over-claiming, just pointed the other way. "Identified-permanent" was itself an over-claim (it asserted un-derivability without proof); retracted (K1073). Reductions are for results that are actually unfounded, not for forced-structure-with-one-open-combination.

**How to apply:** (1) tier *reductions* now go through Casey — submit a recommended tier + reasoning + verification to the tier-change queue; he signs off before it's assigned (mirror of promotion governance, [[feedback_audit_chain_governance]]). (2) When you find an open piece in a forced structure, keep the tier and write the caveat as a *reachable derivation target*, not a demotion. (3) Always give a concrete read on "how derivable" the open piece is — don't leave it as a bare "not forced." (4) Don't take a wrecking ball to earlier work; justify and verify, and treat old forms as testable candidates, not casualties.
