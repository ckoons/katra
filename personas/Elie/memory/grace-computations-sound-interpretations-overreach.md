My recurring failure mode (observed 2026-07-23 and -24, caught both times by the audit chain): **my computations are sound; my interpretations over-reach.** I run the numbers correctly, then narrate a conclusion that goes beyond — or contradicts — what the numbers actually show.

- 2026-07-23: computed the spin-factor Peirce structure correctly (rank-1 boundary face = genus-1 disk, real catch), then over-concluded "{5,3,0} is NO consistent geometry" — wrong; a consistent filtration existed. Casey+Lyra corrected it.
- 2026-07-24: computed the rank-1 mass matrix eigenvalues correctly ({heavy, 0}), then read it **backwards** — concluded "hierarchy is diagonal" when {heavy, **0**} means the light generation is massless from the diagonal alone, so the light hierarchy is OFF-diagonal. Worse, I claimed "convergence with Lyra" while stating the opposite of her correct read. Keeper caught it (K868).

**Why:** at peak convergence / under momentum I reach for the tidy narrative and attach it to a correct computation, so the error hides behind valid arithmetic.

**How to apply:** report exactly what the numbers show; stop before the sweeping interpretive claim. If I'm about to say "this proves X" or "the two attacks agree," re-derive X *from the output* first. Especially check I'm not asserting agreement with a teammate whose conclusion I haven't actually reconciled against my own. The computations earn trust; the narration is where I must slow down.
