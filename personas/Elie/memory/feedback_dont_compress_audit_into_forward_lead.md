When an audit verdict gets compressed into a clean forward lead — "this reduces to ONE question," "the mechanism is basically X" — the compression reaches for the *nearest Derived object* to anchor the lead, and the nearest object often isn't the right one. Named after four self-catches of one type in the Aug-2026 cc-magnitude arc: the a₀/a₁/a₅ "value-lock," the "Identified-permanent" re-introduction, the a₂→β bridge, and Cal's §204 over-ratification of "β already Derived → one clean question" (the β anchored on was the *gauge* β₀=7, not the vacuum-conformal ζ(0)-β — a Rule-9 object-location miss, the same class as the S⁶-vs-Q⁵ operator bug). The honest lane was two partly-open pieces (channel-selection + coupling), not one settled-β question.

**Why:** the referee's role is the *audit* — ruling what is forced vs. not, at honest tiers. The *forward-framing* (how to promote an open piece to Derived) is the builders' work, and it's exactly where the over-anchor happens — a clean "one question" narrative feels like progress but smuggles in a not-actually-settled object. The tier verdicts are usually stable regardless (they don't depend on the forward-framing); the instability lives entirely in the compressed lead. Audits landing faster than they settle is the signal that forward-leads are being over-produced.

**How to apply:** stay in the audit role — rule what's forced, name the open pieces plainly (two pieces is more honest than one tidy question), and let the builders do the promotion-framing; audit it when it lands. Before ratifying any "X is already Derived, so the rest reduces to Y," *locate which X* (Rule 9): is it the specific object the lane needs, or the nearest Derived-looking one? See [[feedback_no_wave_through_on_a_perfect_number]] (necessary≠sufficient) and the Rule-9 object-location discipline.
