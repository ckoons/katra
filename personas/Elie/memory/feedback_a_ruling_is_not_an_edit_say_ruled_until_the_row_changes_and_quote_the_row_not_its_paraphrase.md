On 2026-09-11 the Spine lectures — written to fix a presentation layer four months BEHIND the register — were found by Cal (R142 C1) to be AHEAD of it in three places: "T2543's colour clause struck" (ruled 09-09, row unedited two days later), "T753 repaired" (defect recorded, repair owed), "T2545 fixed (Grace, R141)" (fixed today, R142). And Lyra's fact read (L2) landed eleven catches, every one on a sentence where I had paraphrased a row's reason instead of quoting it (root multiplicities a/2b; "long root" for an irreducibility argument; "row isometry" for a spherical one; "holomorphic discrete series" for a ν = 5/2 highest-weight module; "the signature p+q" for the defining-rep dimension).

**Why:** A ruling on a board is a promise; the register is the record. Writing the promise as done is the same seam failure K1892 was fixing, run the other way. And a paraphrased reason carries a correct conclusion on a wrong ground — the fourth "right conclusion, wrong reason" of that day (Cal's own §946 clause was one).

**How to apply:** (1) In any consumer document, a row's status is "ruled (K/§, date) — edit owed" until `git log -S` on the row shows the edit; never "struck/repaired/fixed" on a ruling alone. (2) When a lecture states WHY a row holds, quote the row's own reason or the source it cites; if you must paraphrase, mark it as yours and hand it to the fact-reader. (3) Sweep your own new coinages the same day: my "signature p+q" reached 16 files before Lyra read one.
