across the D_IV^n family before proposing one as a BST signature.

Two caught the same day (Elie 5336, 5340, 2026-08-17):
- **Kostant constant** ‖ρ_G‖² − ‖ρ_K‖² = 25/4 for D_IV⁵ — but it is **(n/2)² identically in n**
  (verified n = 3…10).
- **F64's coefficient** |ℓ_B/a| = √(π⁵/5) — but the form **√(π^n/n) holds for every n**.
- Same category as the earlier **c₂ = dim K identically in n**, and **R = −n** for the bulk curvature.

So "is the 5 the domain's n_C?" can be answered **yes, and it is forced, and it still selects
nothing** — the identity holds equally at n = 3, 4, 6, 7.

**Why:** forced ≠ meaningful. A family identity wearing a clean number passes every check aimed at
coincidence-hunting, because it genuinely is not a coincidence — it is just not *about* D_IV⁵.

**How to apply:** sweep first, propose second. Report dimension-generic identities as such and do not
bank them. Pairs with [[feedback_no_wave_through_on_a_perfect_number]] and
