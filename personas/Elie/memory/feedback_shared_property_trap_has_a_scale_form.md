The program's standing catch — [[feedback_ingredient_passes_application_smuggles]], a shared integer
is not a shared object — has a **units-and-scales form** that fires the same way.

Elie 5338 (2026-08-17): the **Koons commitment tick** and the **gravitational UV cutoff** are both
"the shortest time in the theory." Nominating the tick as the cutoff for Sakharov induced gravity
gives G too small by **α^{2C₂} = α^72 ≈ 10⁻¹⁵⁴**. Same object-name, different physical roles.

A companion distinction survived the kill: the tick as a **unit anchor** (a choice of units) was
*not* refuted — only the tick as a **dynamical cutoff** inside the heat-kernel integral. One object,
two roles; refuting one role is not refuting the other.

**Why:** dimensional quantities acquire meaning from the *equation they sit in*, not from their
description. "The smallest time" is a description, not a role.

**How to apply:** when a scale is nominated for a job, name the equation it enters and check the
role, not the adjective. And before proposing a clean dimensionful relation, run the
family/dimension sweep first — see [[feedback_sweep_the_family_before_calling_a_clean_number_a_signature]].
