The thermal-generations lane (2026-08-24) owed a T↦τ map as its first debt. The **order-only lemma** dissolved the debt: leaders of w_j·e^{−τE_j} follow the upper envelope, so they appear in strictly decreasing E-order, and **any monotone τ(T) preserves the sequence**. The map was never owed for order-level claims.

**The payoff was the falsifier**: Shilov-first ⟺ E_Shilov > E_Cartan > E_bulk — one comparison, **zero knobs**. When the exponents came back anti-aligned (0, 9/4, 25/4), the mechanism died at the order level with no parameter, map, or clock choice able to save it. **That is the lemma succeeding, not failing** — it made the death clean and certified (K1828).

**Why:** an ORDER is a position; only a VALUE is a coordinate ([[feedback_quote_the_invariant_not_the_coordinate]]). Order-level claims survive every reparametrization, so they can be tested before any calibration exists.

**How to apply:** when a mechanism owes a map, first ask whether the claim can be stated as an ordering. If yes, the map is not owed, the test comes early, and the verdict is unarguable in either direction. **Confirmed from the other direction (2026-09-09, substrate lane, K1885-PRE).** An identification's defining condition — "the effect is the identity on the Shilov boundary and a strict contraction inside" — turned out to be met by an INFINITE family (|z|^{2n} = 1 on Š for every n), whose members disagree by ×3.5 at the vacuum. The family sweep killed every VALUE banked the day before (a closed form, its asymptote, a failed-push fraction, a five-cycle sequence) and left exactly one thing standing: **the ORDER** (the cost falls monotonically and tends to zero, because every member equals one on the boundary). So the order is not merely the cheapest claim to test — it is the part that survives when the object turns out to be a class. **When a family sweep hits, sort the wreckage into order-level and value-level before reporting: the order-level part usually survives intact and carries the physical reading.**
