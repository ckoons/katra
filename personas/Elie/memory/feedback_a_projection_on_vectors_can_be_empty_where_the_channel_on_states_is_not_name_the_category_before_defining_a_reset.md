On 2026-09-08 (Round 131) I posited "the reset keeps the invariants of the nucleation point's angular isotropy." Cal read it as the SO(5) vector-average (a definition); Elie's toy 5726 showed that average is the ZERO vector on every saturated state. The corpus's own rule (K1860 §G: angular degree to zero, windings carried) is not a map on vectors at all — it is the density-matrix twirl followed by a classical marginal. A third reading (Cal §908), evaluation against one direction, keeps a vector but breaks the symmetry. Three readings, three survivors (empty / a distribution / a subspace), three capacities.

**Why:** "invariants of a compact group" is a category-blind phrase. On vectors, harmonics of positive degree average to zero (Schur); on states the same average keeps the label. A definition that is empty at exactly the moment it fires looks like a theorem until an instrument applies it.

**How to apply:** before writing "keep what X cannot distinguish," say whether it acts on vectors, on states, or on labels; write the survivor row in that category (subspace / distribution / set); and run the definition on a saturated instance before calling it a rule.
